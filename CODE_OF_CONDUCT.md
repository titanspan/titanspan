# Code of Conduct

Titanspan is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussion and review.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation,
and publishing another person's private information without permission are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates this
policy. Report behavior using GitHub's report-content controls or by
contacting a repository administrator through GitHub. Reports should include
links and enough context to investigate; do not post private information
publicly.

This policy applies to organization repositories and project spaces maintained
by Titanspan.
